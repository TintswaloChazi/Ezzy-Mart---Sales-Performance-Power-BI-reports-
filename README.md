# Ezzy-Mart---Sales-Performance-Power-BI-reports-

Project Overview:
This interactive PowerBI sales dashboard was developed to analyse Ezzy Mart's sales and profitability performance from 2020 to 2023. The. dashboard provides insights into sales trends, product and category performance, profitability by country and by store, carrier performance and shipping methods.

Project Objective:
The objective of this project was to transform raw sales data using Power Query into an interactive dashboard that supports data driven decision making.

Tools used:
- PowerBI
- PowerQuery
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

Skills demonstrated:
- Data cleaning
- Data transformation
- Data Loading
- DAX measures
- Data visualisation
- Dashboard design

Key insights:
- Overall performance: Ezzy Mart generated in total sales, total profit and the resulting overall profit ratio.
- Performance over time: Sales have increased across the period displayed, showing 2023 as the strongest overall sales performance.
- Product & Category Performance: Technology has contributed the largest share of sales at 38,47% while the product ranking highlights the products driving the highest sales.
- Store performance: The analysis shows a positive relationship between store ssales and profit, allowing high and lower performing stores to be identified
- Geographical performance: Profit ratios vary across countries, allowing us to identify more and less profitable markets from a geographic perspective.
- Shipping & Carrier analysis: The report highlights how sales are distributed across shipping modes and carriers, including the concentration of sales handled by different carriers.
- Interactive analysis: year and state slicers allow us to dynamically analyse performance for speciic periods and locatinons, making it possible for us to compare sales and profitability acros different years and geographic arears.
